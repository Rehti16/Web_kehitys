Kyoto, Japan

Kyoto Travel Notes

Where do i even start with Kyoto, one of the most beautiful cities in the world. I've been to many places in the world and seen many beautiful cities but Kyoto is on a whole different level. The culture, the food, the people, the temples, the shrines, the gardens, the bamboo forest, the mountains, the river, the city itself, everything is just so beautiful. The best part is that it's not just Kyoto itself but the whole country of Japan is just so beautiful and Kyoto is the perfect place to experience it all. And from Kyoto you can easily take day trips to other beautiful places in Japan like Nara, Osaka, Hiroshima, Tokyo, Himeji and many more. I would recommend everyone to take that Japan trip out of the group chat and make it a reality, best trip of my entire life.
