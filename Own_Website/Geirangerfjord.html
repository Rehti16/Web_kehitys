Geirangerfjord, Norway

About Geirangerfjord

Me and my family were desperately searching for a place with mountains and water and we found this place and decided to drive there for summers holidays. And it was such a breathtaking view and the combination of the mountains and the water was just amazing. there was also a lot of waterfalls and the nature was just so beautiful. I would recommend this place to anyone who loves nature and wants to experience one of the most beautiful underrated locations in the world.
