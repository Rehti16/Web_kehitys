Sydney, Australia

Sydney Travel Notes

I myself went to Sydney to get my first experience of a very big city and to see the highly talked Sydney new years fireworks. And Sydney did not disappoint. The city is so vibrant and full of life and there is always something to do. I loved the mix of nature and city life in Sydney. You can go to the beach and then go to a bar in the city in the same day. And the views from the city are just breathtaking. Fireworks also were amazing and I would recommend anyone to go to Sydney for new years if they want to experience Close second to the best new years celebrations behind Japan.

Would you go spend your new years in sydney to witness the amazing fireworks?
