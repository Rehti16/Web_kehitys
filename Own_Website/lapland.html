Lapland, Finland

Lapland Travel Notes

Okay I know I mentioned I live in Finland but I just had to include Lapland in this list because it is such a unique and beautiful place. I have been to Lapland many times and every time I go there I am amazed by the beauty of the nature and the peacefulness of the place. If you want to experience a magical winter wonderland and see the northern lights, Lapland is the place to go. I would recommend this place to anyone who loves nature and wants to experience something truly unique.
