The Alps, Austria

Trip Reflection

I've always loved nature and especially mountains so of course I had to get to the Alps. But even though I love mountains and nature, I still have a very bad fear of heights so getting up there needed a lot of courage gathering. I'm so glad that I ended up facing my fears and going anyway because the clarity of the air and the sheer magnitude of the landscape completely changed my perspective on natures beauty.

Would you be interested to head to the mountains and experience this breathtaking view?
